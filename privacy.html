Privacy Policy - Video Uploader

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What the app does

Video Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API, and to read the owner's own YouTube Analytics reports.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uploads videos, captions and thumbnails, sets video details (title, description, tags, privacy and schedule), reads the channel name to confirm the correct channel, and reads analytics reports for the owner's own videos. It does not access any other user's data.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
